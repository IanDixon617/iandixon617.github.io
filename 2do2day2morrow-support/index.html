Support & FAQ

How do I use natural language input?
Just type naturally. Try things like "lunch tomorrow at noon," "pick up milk Friday," or "call mom next week." The app learns the date and time automatically. If it doesn't get it quite right, you can edit it in the task details.
What's the difference between free and paid?
Free gives you: Today, Tomorrow, and Completed views. Unlock Pro adds: Soon (undated tasks), Groups (organize by project/context), Plan (400-day agenda view), and custom themes with your own backgrounds.
Does my data sync to the cloud?
No. Your data stays entirely on your phone. There's no account, no sync, no cloud. It's yours alone.
Can I back up my tasks?
Not yet, but it's coming. For now, keep your iPhone backup current—your 2do data is included in iOS backups.
How do I attach photos?
Long-press any task and select "Add photo." Useful for capturing reference materials, receipts, or anything visual you need to remember.
Can I share tasks or lists?
Yes. Swipe left on a task to share it via Messages, Mail, or other apps.
Do you have a privacy policy?
Yes. Read our privacy policy here. TL;DR: no tracking, no ads, no data collection.
What's "Plan mode"?
It's a 400-day calendar view where you can see your full agenda at once. Perfect for long-term planning or spotting busy periods ahead. (Paid feature.)
Can I edit or delete tasks?
Absolutely. Tap any task to edit it. Swipe left to delete or move it to another date.
I found a bug or have a feature idea.
Great. Reach out via email and let me know. Bug reports with screenshots help a lot.
