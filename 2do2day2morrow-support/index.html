Support & FAQ

How do I use natural language input?
Just type naturally. Try things like "lunch tomorrow at noon," "pick up milk Friday," or "call mom next Monday." The app learns the date and time automatically. If it doesn't get it quite right, you can edit it in the task details.
What's the difference between free and paid?
Free: Today, Tomorrow, Soon (the next two weeks), Shelved (tasks with no date), Lists, Completed, reminders, daily and weekly repeats, photos and notes on tasks, and export.

Unlock Pro (one-off £1.99, no subscription): Plan (400-day agenda view), Groups, "My theme" (your own photos as backgrounds), and monthly and yearly repeats.
Does my data sync to the cloud?
No. Your data stays entirely on your phone. There's no account, no sync, no cloud. It's yours alone.
Can I back up my tasks?
Yes. Go to Settings and tap "Export my tasks" to save your tasks as a text file via the share sheet. Your 2do data is also included in normal iPhone backups, so keep those current.
How do I attach photos?
Long-press a task to open the editor, then tap "Add photo." Useful for capturing reference materials, receipts, or anything visual you need to remember.
Can I share tasks or lists?
Yes. Long-press a task to open the editor, then tap Share to send it via Messages, Mail, or other apps.
Do you have a privacy policy?
Yes. Read our privacy policy here. TL;DR: no tracking, no ads, no data collection.
What's "Plan mode"?
It's a 400-day calendar view where you can see your full agenda at once. Perfect for long-term planning or spotting busy periods ahead. (Paid feature.)
Can I edit or delete tasks?
Absolutely. Long-press a task to open the editor, where you can change anything or delete it. To move a task to another day, use the chevron menu on the task.
I found a bug or have a feature idea.
Great. Reach out via email and let me know. Bug reports with screenshots help a lot.
